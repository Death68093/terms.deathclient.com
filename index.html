DeathClient Terms & Privacy

This page explains the rules for using DeathClient and how account information, cloud saves, player data, and cosmetics are handled.

Last updated: September 22, 2026
Important information

This document describes the DeathClient service and the account/cloud-saving system represented by the current server implementation. It is intended to explain the service clearly; it is not a substitute for legal advice.

Privacy Policy

DeathClient respects the privacy of its users. The account system is designed primarily to provide authentication and cloud-saving functionality.

Information associated with an account is used to provide the DeathClient services described on this page. DeathClient does not intentionally collect information that is not needed for those services through the account API.

Information We Store

When you create and use a DeathClient account, the service may store the following categories of information.

Username

Your chosen DeathClient username is stored so that your account can be identified and authenticated.

Password Hash

Your password is hashed before being stored. E.g "password123" may become: "$2y$12$5cm1nT9K4Z95yr8PGtiJwOFN/5gjCbKOWwzO3GVXpDVbfnWD1zzZq"

Cosmetics

Unlocked account cosmetics, including supported capes and other account cosmetics, may be stored with your account.

Player Data

Your cloud profile, settings, statistics, achievements, and cosmetic data may be stored as part of cloud saving.

Cloud World

You may store one cloud world through the provided world-saving system.

World Metadata

The server stores information such as the world name, file size, and the time at which the world was saved.

Information Not Included in the Account Database

Based on the server implementation provided for this policy, the account database does not ask users for their real name, postal address, telephone number, date of birth, or email address.

The application account database also does not store plaintext passwords.

Network infrastructure, hosting providers, security services, or web infrastructure used to operate DeathClient may independently process technical information such as network requests, connection metadata, or IP addresses. Such processing can depend on the infrastructure used to deliver the service and may be governed by the relevant provider's own policies.

Password Security

DeathClient does not store account passwords in plaintext.

When an account is created, the supplied password is processed using bcrypt with a work factor of 12 before it is stored.

During authentication, the supplied password is compared against the stored bcrypt hash rather than being compared with a plaintext password.

Protect your password

Do not reuse your DeathClient password on other websites or services. DeathClient cannot protect accounts on unrelated services using the same password.

Cloud Saving

DeathClient provides cloud-saving features that allow account-associated game information to be stored on the DeathClient server.

Player Data

The current service can store the following player-data categories:

  • Profile information
  • Client and gameplay settings
  • Cosmetic information
  • Statistics
  • Achievements

Cloud Worlds

Authenticated users may upload a world to the DeathClient cloud-saving system.

The current server implementation applies a maximum cloud-world size of 64 MB per account.

The server stores the world as an EPK file and records metadata including its name, size, and save time.

World storage
64 MB
Backup quota
32 MB

The server also provisions a separate 32 MB backup-storage quota per account. The exact use of that area may depend on future or server-side backup functionality.

Cosmetics & Capes

DeathClient may provide account cosmetics such as capes.

Cosmetic ownership is associated with your account and may be synchronized with your cloud player data.

The server also supports dynamically configured cape assets. Cape images are served as PNG files and are subject to a maximum file size of 2 MB per configured cape.

How Your Information Is Used

Account information is used to operate DeathClient features such as:

  • Creating and authenticating your account
  • Loading and saving cloud player data
  • Saving and loading your cloud world
  • Remembering unlocked account cosmetics
  • Reporting cloud-storage usage

DeathClient does not need your stored player information to provide unrelated services.

Data Sharing

DeathClient does not intentionally sell your account information.

Your account data is used to provide the DeathClient services associated with your account. DeathClient may need to rely on hosting, infrastructure, networking, security, or other technical service providers to operate its systems.

DeathClient may also disclose information when required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

Cookies & Tracking

The authentication server shown in the current implementation does not create an application login session using a server-side cookie.

Authentication requests are handled directly by the DeathClient client and authentication API.

The website or infrastructure used to serve DeathClient may use cookies, security mechanisms, analytics, caching, or similar technologies independently of the authentication API. Such technologies should be documented separately if they are enabled.

Data Retention

Account information and cloud-saving data may remain associated with your account while the account is active and the service continues to provide cloud-saving functionality.

Cloud worlds can be deleted through the corresponding cloud-world deletion functionality. Deleting a saved world removes the stored world file and its associated world metadata.

At present, the server implementation provided for this policy does not expose a public self-service account-deletion endpoint.

For account-related deletion or privacy requests, contact: biz@deathclient.com

Security

DeathClient uses several technical controls to protect account data, including password hashing, authenticated endpoints, filesystem path validation, storage limits, and atomic writes for important JSON data.

Password verification uses bcrypt rather than plaintext password storage.

Cloud-world and player-data endpoints require successful authentication before account data can be accessed.

No system is guaranteed to be perfectly secure

DeathClient cannot guarantee that its infrastructure will never experience a vulnerability, outage, unauthorized access, corruption, or other security incident.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does DeathClient store my password?

DeathClient stores a bcrypt password hash rather than your plaintext password.

What does cloud saving store?

Cloud saving may store your profile, settings, cosmetics, statistics, achievements, and cloud-world data.

Can I delete my cloud world?

Yes. The current service provides a cloud-world deletion operation that removes the stored world and its associated metadata.

Can I delete my entire account?

The current authentication API provided for this policy does not include a public self-service account deletion endpoint. Contact biz@deathclient.com for an account-data request.

Does DeathClient sell my account information?

DeathClient does not intentionally sell account information.

Are my cloud saves guaranteed to be permanent?

No. Cloud storage is a convenience feature and should not be treated as your only backup.
